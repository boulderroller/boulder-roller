05 — Action Hierarchy

Orange for primary actions (stake, claim, upload). Gold-tinted for secondary. Ghost for tertiary. Creates clear visual hierarchy and adds energy.

  • The Boulder — Glowing orb as signature element. Appears in nav, cards, loading, achievements.
  • Topographic texture — Subtle contour lines whisper "terrain" in backgrounds.
  • Orange + Gold duality — Gold for passive/earned, Orange for action/energy.
  • Data sparklines — Mini charts everywhere. TrainingPeaks credibility.
  • Beetle card as hero — Shell shape, rolling boulder, coherence bar. Unmistakably Dung Beetle.
