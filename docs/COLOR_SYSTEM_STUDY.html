Comparison Notes

Phosphor Green

Most "tool-like". Strong hacker/terminal association. Very high contrast and legibility. Risk: could read as "trying to be Matrix" if overdone. Best for: power users who want to feel like operators.

Electric Violet

Most distinctive. Zero association with cycling or crypto. Premium, mysterious, slightly alien. Risk: might feel "too fashion" for fitness audience. Best for: standing out completely, attracting design-forward users.

Plasma Cyan

Most balanced. Technical but not cold. Medical/aerospace precision. Very readable, good contrast. Risk: safer choice, less memorable. Best for: broad appeal, professional credibility.

Hot Coral

Most human. Warm, energetic, alive. Completely unexpected for cycling. Risk: might read as "social app" rather than "serious tool". Best for: approachability, standing out from dark/cold competitors.
