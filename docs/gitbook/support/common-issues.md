# Common issues

## Sign-in

**Signed in but your rides are missing.** Sign out and back in with the method
you used to create the account. The web offers email and wallet sign-in; if
you created your account with Apple, sign in on iOS. Wallet users: unlock the
wallet and reconnect.

## Rides

**No climbs matched.** Most rides cross no defined climb. Climbs are defined
only in and around Philadelphia so far. If the ride did cross one, check that
it followed the climb's route from start to finish. Uploading the same file
again returns the same ride; if you think a match was missed, contact support
with the ride date.

**Climb ridden, no result.** Processing may be incomplete; refresh after a few
minutes. Otherwise compare the climb's route to the ride. A leaderboard place
also needs a vault on the climb, and waits while the ride is held or under
review.

**Stuck on Counting** (**Processing** on iOS). Wait a few minutes and refresh.
If nothing changes, contact support with the ride date.

**No CAL.** Usually one of these: you rode without a power meter or with too
little power data; your Threshold is not yet established, so the CAL is held
until you record a ride in the iOS app with a power meter
([Threshold](../rides/cal.md#threshold)); or the ride is under review.

**Old ride not counted.** A ride completed more than 30 days before upload is
not written on-chain. It counts no CAL and takes no leaderboard place.

**Ride under review.** A ride under review counts no CAL and takes no
leaderboard place, and review does not clear on its own
([Review](../rides/processing.md#review)). If you think the ride should have
counted, contact support with the ride date.

## DUNG

**No DUNG after a ride.** DUNG settles weekly, not per ride, and only to
leaderboard seats, Local Legends, and backers. Check **Settlements** (iOS) or
**History** in Portfolio (web) from Tuesday 12:00 UTC.

**DUNG ready, not received.** DUNG from deposits requires **Collect**.
Leaderboard and Local Legend DUNG does not.

## iOS

**Recording does not start.** Enable location access in iOS Settings.

**Sensor does not pair.** Enable Bluetooth, wake the sensor, and disconnect it
from any other app or head unit.

**Ride saved on this device.** If the card shows **Review**, tap it and save
the ride to upload it. If it doesn't, the ride was shorter than 100 m or had
fewer than 30 GPS samples and stays on your phone; open it and choose **Export
Ride File**.

Unresolved: [Contact](contact.md).
