# Balance and wallet

| Account | Holds | Purpose |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Wallet | CAL counted from rides; DUNG paid at settlement or collected | Pays for deposits and vault creation |
| Boulder Roller Balance | CAL, USDC, and DUNG you move in | Not open yet |

- CAL counted from a ride is credited to your wallet.
- **Add funds** moves CAL or USDC from your wallet into the Balance; it is not
  open yet.
- **Deposit CAL** into a vault is paid from your wallet; **Withdraw CAL**
  returns CAL to your wallet and is not open yet.
- **Withdraw to wallet** moves Balance holdings back to your wallet; it is not
  open yet.
- **Collect DUNG** sends DUNG directly to your wallet.

Portfolio (web) shows both accounts. If an amount is blank and the heading
reads **Delayed** or **Last loaded**, Boulder Roller could not read Solana
just then; your balance is not zero.
