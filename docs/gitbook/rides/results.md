# Results

A result is your performance on one climb in one ride.

```text
Manayunk Wall      5:42 · 312 W · 7th
Lyceum Avenue      2:18 · 340 W · New best
```

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Time | Start to finish, to the second |
| Power | Your average power over the climb. ⚡ means a power meter measured it. ≈ means we estimated it from your time and the climb, using a standard rider weight. |
| Place | Position of your best time on the climb's leaderboard, when the climb has a vault and your time is in its top ten; tied times share a place |
| Best | Your fastest time on the climb across your own history |
| Top 10 | Your best time currently holds one of the ten leaderboard seats |

The ride also shows **Ride CAL**, the CAL it counted ([CAL](cal.md)).

A ride can set a new best on any climb. On a climb with a vault it can also
enter or move within the top ten and add a completion toward Local Legend; a
held ride does so when it is released. Most rides cross no defined climb and
produce no climb result. Vault deposits do not affect results or places.
