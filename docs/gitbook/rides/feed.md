# Feed and following

The feed is the list of rides, newest first. On the web it has two views:

| View | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Everyone** | Every ride whose audience is Everyone |
| Following | Your rides and the rides of riders you follow |

The iOS app shows the Everyone view only.

## Following

- Follow a rider with **Follow** on their profile or from **Follow picks** on
  the feed. To find a rider, use Search. Following is immediate; there is no
  approval step.
- Unfollow with the same button. A rider is notified when you follow them,
  not when you unfollow.
- Following is one-directional. A rider you follow does not see your
  Followers-only rides unless they follow you back.
- Following is managed on the web and applies to both apps.

## What a follower sees

A follower sees your rides whose audience is Followers or Everyone: the
climbs, results, and the map you chose ([Privacy and your data](privacy.md)).
The app never shows them your raw sensor streams, CAL, or DUNG; what is public
on-chain is listed under [On-chain data](privacy.md#on-chain-data).

## Notifications

**Notifications** on the web lists new followers and other account events.
