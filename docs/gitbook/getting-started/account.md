# Create an account

| Sign-in method | Web | iOS |
|----------------|-----|-----|
| Email, one-time code | Yes | Yes |
| Google | No | No |
| Apple | No | Yes |
| Solana wallet (**Use wallet instead**), for accounts created with Phantom, Backpack, or Solflare | Yes | No |

Email sign-in, and Apple sign-in on iOS, create a Solana wallet bound to your
account. The wallet holds your CAL and DUNG and signs your vault deposits and
withdrawals. Boulder Roller sends DUNG collections for you, with no signature
needed. You never open the wallet to upload rides, see results, or receive
CAL. If you signed up with Phantom, Backpack, or Solflare, Boulder Roller uses
that wallet and creates none.

Boulder Roller never asks for your seed phrase or private key.

## Profile

You set your display name, photo, and weight from your profile; on the web,
**Edit profile** opens Settings. Your Threshold comes from rides you record in
the iOS app with a power meter, and you can't type it in
([Threshold](../rides/cal.md#threshold)).
