# Local Legend

Local Legend is the climb's most frequent recent rider. It is independent of
speed and is decided once per eight-week term, not over a rolling window. Only
a climb with a vault has one.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Term | Eight settlement weeks (56 days) |
| Elected by | Most completions of the climb during the term |
| Tie | The rider whose first completion in the term came earliest |
| Share | 3% of the climb's weekly DUNG, paid at settlement, no fee |

## Rules

- The rider with the most completions in a term holds the next term; the
  current Local Legend keeps the seat by leading again. The result is settled
  with the first week of the new term, about eight days after the term ends.
- A term with no completions leaves the seat empty. The 3% carries forward
  for the next Local Legend and is not paid to anyone else.
- The founder holds the first term, starting when the vault is created; the
  climb page shows it as **Founder stewardship**.
- Stepping down before the term ends is not available in the app yet.
