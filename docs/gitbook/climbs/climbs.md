# Climbs

A climb is a named hill with a fixed start, route, and finish. Boulder Roller
builds each climb from OpenStreetMap roads and elevation data and reviews it
before it goes live. Climbs are currently defined in and around Philadelphia.
Your ride is evidence that you rode a climb and how fast; it never creates
or changes one.

| Component | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| Leaderboard | Top ten by best time, from when the vault was created ([Leaderboards](leaderboards.md)) |
| KOM (King of the Mountain) | The fastest time; riders sharing it to the second are co-KOMs |
| Local Legend | The rider holding the current eight-week term, decided by most completions in the term before it ([Local Legend](local-legend.md)) |
| Vault | Where CAL is deposited and DUNG is settled ([Vaults](vaults.md)) |
| Founder | The rider who created the climb's vault |

## Rules

- A ride produces a result on a climb only when it crosses the start, follows
  the route, and crosses the finish, in that order.
- Riding a hill that is not a defined climb nominates it. Hills nominated by
  at least two riders are queued for consideration as new climbs. A nomination
  alone creates no climb, vault, or DUNG.
- A climb's leaderboard and DUNG begin when it has a vault. Without one, the
  climb records your time and best but shows no leaderboard.
- When a climb's course changes (start, finish, or route), a new course
  version begins with a fresh leaderboard. The earlier course and its
  leaderboard stay readable. Minor corrections that move nothing measured keep
  the leaderboard.

## In the app

**Map** shows every climb. A climb's page shows its route; when the climb has
a vault it also shows the top ten, the current Local Legend, deposited CAL,
and **DUNG history**, one row per settled week.
