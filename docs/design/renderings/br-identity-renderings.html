What changes

Clean athletic first. BR second, but unmistakable.

The record surface gets quieter and more confident. The route map is the hero, stats are easy to scan, climb rows look like sport results, and proof/economy is compressed into receipt-like modules.

This direction removes generic crypto-dashboard energy without hiding the core novelty. CAL, vaults, legends, and scan links stay present, but they attach to ride and climb objects.

Maps, elevation, leaderboards, and stats do the visual work.
Proof is a quiet trust marker until the rider asks for detail.
Portfolio stays object-backed: positions belong to climbs.
Mechanical UI becomes emphasis, not the whole costume.
