## Main Launch Thread (Token-Dense)

*Voice: Coach meets Cartographer — explain mechanics, plant seeds, let strategy emerge*

### Tweet 1 (Hook)
```
Top 20 at USA Cycling crit nationals. Coached 5 years at Cadence Cycling, wrote their workout library. Analyzed thousands of power files. Probably more hours on Strava than anyone you know.

I spend hours every week flagging fake rides to keep their leaderboards honest. For free.

```

### Tweet 2 (Problem)
```
Strava has 120M users. Billions of segments.

All created by athletes. All owned by Strava.

They can delete your segments. Change the rules. Ban your account. Modify leaderboards. Raise prices. Sell your data.

You built their most valuable asset. You get a badge and a subscription fee.
```

### Tweet 3 (Solution)
```
I'm building @BoulderRoller.

Segments on-chain. Permanent. Can't be deleted. Can't be modified without governance. Can't be taken from the community.

Power data required. No fake rides. No GPS spoofing. No motor doping the leaderboard.

No subscription. Ever.

The map belongs to the athletes.
```

### Tweet 4 (Mechanics)
```
How it works:

Upload a ride. Power meter required. Earn tokens based on effort—your Training Stress Score becomes value. Your ride distributes that value to every segment you crossed, weighted by intensity.

Segments accumulate. Weekly epochs pay out: 70% to top 10 riders, 20% to stakers, 10% to curators.

Ride hard. Earn more.
```

### Tweet 5 (Staking - seeds planted, not harvested)
```
Every segment is a vault.

Riders fill it with effort. Stakers earn yield from traffic. You can stake without riding—passive income from roads you believe in.

Some roads are quiet. Some aren't. Some come alive in certain seasons. Some are contested every weekend.

The map has geography. Geography has value.
```

### Tweet 6 (Anti-cheat)
```
"What about cheaters?"

Power data required. A power meter costs $300-1000. That's the gate. Natural sybil resistance.

GPS is easy to fake. Heart rate varies wildly. Power is physics. Watts are watts.

No spoofing. No fake rides. No motor doping. Can't produce watts? Can't earn.

The barrier becomes the moat.
```

### Tweet 7 (Analytics)
```
Power data unlocks what Strava won't build:

w/kg leaderboards—not just who's fastest, who's strongest pound-for-pound. Your power curve vs the KOM holder—see exactly where you're losing. "At your FTP, you'd do X:XX"—predicted times for any segment. TSS per segment—where did your training stress come from? Season-long power progression—are you actually getting faster?

We require power. So we can do things they never will.
```

### Tweet 8 (Tokenomics - principles)
```
Economics designed by a cyclist, not VCs:

No subscription. 40% of tokens to the community—the people who actually ride. Achievement gates—can't buy your way to the top, have to earn it on the road. Protocol-owned liquidity from day 1—we own our own market depth.

Steep progression curve. Some riders never reach the top. That's the point.

Built to last. Not to dump.
```

### Tweet 9 (What's next)
```
Points period launching soon.

Earn points now based on your riding. Convert to tokens at TGE. Early riders get the best conversion rates—tiered system protects smaller riders from whale dilution.

Power meter required. This isn't for everyone. It's for serious cyclists.

Follow @BoulderRoller

The athletes who built the map should own it.
```
