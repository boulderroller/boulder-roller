Design Principles

Grey Scale Foundation

13 carefully tuned greys from --grey-950 (deepest) to --grey-50 (lightest). No pure black or white. Slight cool undertone keeps it professional without being cold. The greys do the heavy lifting.

Signal Colors Pop

Five signal colors (mint, violet, coral, amber, cyan) are reserved for data and actions. They're never used for decoration — only to convey meaning. Against the grey foundation, they have room to breathe and communicate.

Semantic Consistency

Mint = fitness/positive. Violet = economy/tokens. Coral = energy/CAL/negative. Amber = time/warning. Cyan = system/info. Users learn these associations unconsciously. The grey lets the signals do their job.

Curve Philosophy

Functional, no-nonsense, information-dense. The interface doesn't try to impress — it tries to inform. The grey scale creates hierarchy through subtle value shifts rather than competing colors. Professional tool energy.
