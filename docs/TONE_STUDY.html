Tone Comparison

Slate Grey (#12141A)

Cool blue undertones. Professional, technical, trustworthy. The Hyperliquid/trading platform aesthetic. Feels like a serious tool. Good for power users.

Warm Charcoal (#141312)

Slight brown/amber undertones. More comfortable, less clinical. Cream text (#FAF8F5) is easier on eyes for long sessions. Feels "lived-in".

Deep Navy (#0C0E14)

Rich blue-black with depth. Premium, sophisticated. Colors pop beautifully — especially mint and coral. Hints of night sky without being void.

Light Mode (#F8F9FC)

Completely different energy. Clean, airy, mainstream. Signal colors darken to maintain contrast. Consider offering as toggle for daytime use.
