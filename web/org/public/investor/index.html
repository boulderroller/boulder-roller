CAL

What you earn from riding. Every watt you push creates CAL. It's proof of effort—like airline miles, but for cycling.

DUNG

What you get paid in. When your backed climbs generate activity, you receive DUNG. This is the currency—like dollars in your account.

1,000 CAL converts to 1 DUNG. Effort in, value out.
